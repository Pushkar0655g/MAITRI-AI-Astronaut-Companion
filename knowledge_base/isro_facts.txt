The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) is the national space agency of India. Its headquarters are in the city of Bengaluru.

Gaganyaan is an ongoing Indian Human Spaceflight Programme. The primary objective is to launch a crew of 3 members to an orbit of 400 km for a 3 days mission and bring them back safely to Earth.

Chandrayaan-3 was an ISRO mission that successfully landed on the south pole of the Moon on August 23, 2023. The lander is named Vikram and the rover is named Pragyan.
